3. Sensitive information

Some of what a dating profile naturally contains is treated as a special category under laws such as the UK/EU GDPR and India's DPDP Act — in particular religion, sexual orientation (implied by who you ask to be shown), and health-adjacent lifestyle answers such as diet, drinking, and smoking.

Our legal basis for these is your explicit consent, given by choosing to enter them. They are entirely optional, they are used only for matching and your own filters, and you can clear any of them at any time from Profile → Edit. Clearing a field removes it from your profile and from future match scoring.

4. Location

If you grant location permission, we take your coordinates on the device and immediately round them to a grid cell roughly one kilometre across. Only that cell is sent to our servers. Your precise coordinates never leave your phone, and they are not stored anywhere.

Other members are shown an approximate distance in kilometres. They are never shown your cell, your address, or a point on a map. You can revoke location permission in your phone's settings at any time; the app keeps working, but distance-based sorting and filters stop being meaningful.

5. Selfie verification

No biometrics are stored

Verification compares a live selfie against your profile photos to confirm you are the same person. When it finishes, the only thing written to your account is a single true/false flag. The selfie is not kept, not added to your profile, not shown to anyone, and no face template, faceprint, or other biometric identifier is created or retained.

6. What other members can see

  • Visible on your profile: your first name, age, city, photos, voice intro, bio, prompts, interests, and any optional fields you filled in.
  • Approximate: distance from the viewer.
  • Never visible: your email, phone number, date of birth, exact location, who you passed on, who you blocked, who you reported, and any report you file.

Blocking is silent by design. Someone you block simply stops appearing for you and you stop appearing for them; they are never told a block happened. You can review and undo your blocks in Settings → Block list.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your profile and photos — until you delete your account.
  • Messages — until you delete your account or unmatch. Unmatching deletes the conversation for both people.
  • Likes and passes — up to 12 months, so we stop showing you profiles you already decided on.
  • Blocks — kept even after account deletion, in a form that identifies neither person's profile content, so that a blocked person cannot return by making a new account.
  • Reports and safety records — up to 24 months after resolution, because a pattern across time is often the only way to catch a repeat offender.
  • Payment records — as long as tax and accounting law requires.

9. Your rights and controls

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights. We honour all of them for everyone, regardless of location.

  • Access — get a copy of your data.
  • Correction — fix anything wrong. Most fields are editable directly in Profile → Edit.
  • Deletion — see §10.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable file.
  • Withdraw consent — clear any optional or sensitive field at any time.
  • Object or restrict — ask us to stop a particular use.
  • Complain — to your local data protection authority. In the UK that is the ICO; in the EU, your national authority; in India, the Data Protection Board.

To exercise any of these, email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We respond within 30 days.

10. Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account, inside the app. No email, no form, no retention offer you have to argue your way past.

Deletion removes your profile, photos, voice intro, messages, likes, and matches. It takes effect immediately in the app and completes in our backups within 30 days. The narrow exceptions are listed in §8 — chiefly block records and unresolved safety reports, both of which exist to protect other people from you being able to start over.

11. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS); all data is encrypted at rest.
  • Access is enforced per-row in the database itself, not just in the app, so a bug in the client cannot expose another member's data.
  • Photos live in a private bucket and are served only through short-lived signed links.
  • Only a small number of staff can access production data, and only for support and safety work.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator within the timeframes the law requires.

12. Age requirement
