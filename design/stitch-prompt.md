# Master Google Stitch Prompt: DostiConnect (High-Intent Matchmaking for South Asians)

Use this prompt in **Google Stitch** to generate production-ready mobile UI screens, web landing pages, and component design tokens.

---

## 📋 Copy & Paste Prompt for Google Stitch

```text
Design a luxury, high-fidelity mobile application and responsive web experience for "DostiConnect" — the premier high-intent dating & matchmaking platform for modern South Asian (Desi & NRI) singles across India, the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

### 🌟 Brand Positioning & Core Concept
- **Product Type**: Curated deck matchmaking (Hinge + Dil Mil parity with fair monetization).
- **Core Proposition**: "Serious about finding your Jodi." Intentional dating with rich prompt cards, specific likes with comments, direct "Requests", and a 7-point cultural compatibility engine.
- **Aesthetic**: "Midnight Aurora & Champagne Gold" — Ultra-premium, warm, cinematic dark mode. Think five-star luxury hotel lounge meets high-end modern lifestyle, NOT neon hookup apps.
- **Tone**: Romantic, authentic, culturally resonant, respectful, and safe.

---

### 🎨 Design System & Color Tokens
- **Background (`bg`)**: #0B0D14 (Near-black navy night canvas)
- **Card Surfaces (`card`)**: #12151E (Glassmorphism dark card with 1px border #232838)
- **Raised Surfaces (`card2`)**: #1A1E2A (Chips, inputs, floating controls)
- **Primary Accent (`gold`)**: #FF7A2E to #D4AF6A (Warm sunset orange / champagne gold for primary CTAs and active states)
- **Signature Gradient (`grad`)**: Linear gradient from #FF9F1C (Warm Amber) → #FF5E3A (Coral) → #FF2D55 (Ruby Rose)
- **Verified / Live (`emerald`)**: #2EE6D6 / #14B8AE (Teal blue for verified checkmarks and online indicators)
- **Text Ink**: #E9E7E2 (Warm ivory) for primary text; #8B90A3 for secondary metadata
- **Typography**:
  - Display/Headlines: "Unbounded" or "Playfair Display" (Bold, elegant, expressive)
  - UI/Body: "Plus Jakarta Sans" or "Inter" (Clean, modern geometric sans)
- **Geometry**: 24px card radius, 999px pill buttons, subtle glass backdrop-blur (20px), soft multi-layered shadows.

---

### 📱 Key Mobile Screens to Generate (390×844 Canvas)

1. **Screen 1: Curated Match Deck (Discover Screen)**
   - One full profile at a time on an edge-to-edge 3:4 portrait card.
   - Top badges: Blue "Selfie-Verified ✓" badge, "New Here" pill.
   - Name & Age in elegant ivory typography (e.g. "Ananya, 27"), Occupation ("Product Designer @ Tech"), Location ("Bengaluru · Within ~5 km").
   - Cultural & Lifestyle Chips: "💍 Life Partner", "🕉️ Hindu", "🥗 Vegetarian", "Telugu & English".
   - 15-second mini Voice Intro audio player bar with waveform.
   - Interactive Prompts: "My simple pleasures...", "Two truths and a lie...".
   - Bottom Floating Actions: Rewind (undo swipe), Pass (✕ in brick red), Super Like (royal star), Like (♥ in gradient), and Send Request (✉️ direct note).

2. **Screen 2: Deep Profile Builder & 7-Point Compatibility View**
   - 3-6s looping Intro Video header with audio toggle.
   - 7-Point Compatibility breakdown (Social energy, conversation vibe, kids, family orientation, sleep clock, fitness, ideal weekend).
   - "My Basics & Lifestyle" section: Height, Faith, Diet, Drinking, Smoking, Workout, Love Language.

3. **Screen 3: "Likes You" Screen (Fair Free Model)**
   - 2-column grid of admirer photo cards with a gold banner: "12 people like you".
   - Top card unblurred with gold border and badge: "FREE THIS WEEK".
   - Other cards softly blurred with lock icon.
   - Bottom CTA: "Upgrade to Plus — See everyone who likes you".

4. **Screen 4: Direct Requests Inbox**
   - List of personal notes sent by admirers before matching.
   - Each card displays sender avatar, city, quote message ("Loved your note on Big Sur trips!"), with "Decline" and "Accept & Match" buttons.

5. **Screen 5: Real-Time Chat & "It's a Jodi!" Match Celebration**
   - Cinematic celebration modal: Two interlocking gold rings with glowing confetti particles.
   - Realtime messaging thread with attached opening comment, voice note player, photo sharing, and Giphy picker.

6. **Screen 6: Plus Membership & Free Filters**
   - Free vs Plus comparison: Plus unlocks unlimited likes, see who likes you, travel mode in global NRI cities, and 10 daily Requests.
   - 100% Free search filters for religion, language, distance, and relationship goal.

---

### 💻 Web Landing Page Design (Dil Mil & Hinge Style)
- Hero section with bold headline: "Connect on What Matters. Find Your Jodi."
- Floating 3D iPhone mockup displaying the Curated Deck and Requests inbox.
- App Store & Google Play download badges + instant QR code test modal.
- South Asian community highlights: Filter by community, mother tongue, diet, global NRI cities (NYC, London, Toronto, Mumbai, Delhi).
- Real couple success story cards with wedding/engagement photos and quotes.
```
